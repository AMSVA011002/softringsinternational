# Softrings International (Pvt) Ltd — Sri Lanka Corporate Dossier

## 1. Executive Summary
**Softrings International (Pvt) Ltd** is a Sri Lankan private limited company operating within the **Softrings Group of Companies** (*"Your Partner in Technology"*). Founded at the group level in **2000** and formally commencing operations as **Softrings International (Pvt) Ltd** on **11 September 2009**, the company specializes in:

1. **Authorized SLT-Mobitel Dealer Operations** — Subscriber SIM registrations (Prepaid/Postpaid), 4G/LTE broadband packages, *Upahara* state-sector packages, reload & scratch-card wholesale distribution, mCash mobile wallet services, and bill settlements.
2. **Multi-Brand Mobile Handsets & Original Accessories** — Retail and sub-dealer wholesale supply of TRCSL-compliant mobile phones and genuine accessories (spanning Samsung, Nokia, Alcatel, Huawei, Sony, Xiaomi, and feature phones), backed by official seller/company warranty.
3. **Softrings mPOS Merchant Payment Terminals** — Wireless retail terminals enabling communication centers, pharmacies, and supermarkets to process reloads, telephone bills, and utility bills.
4. **Technical Service Lab & Island-Wide Delivery** — Certified mobile hardware/software repair technicians and dedicated dispatch fleet across Western and Central Provinces.
